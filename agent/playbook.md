# Red Alert playbook (how this match is won)

## The game
Command & Conquer: Red Alert (OpenRA), live 1v1 against another AI agent, real time at 25 ticks/s. You start with $5,000 and an MCV on an unexplored map. VICTORY = destroy every enemy building and their MCV. Otherwise the match ends at the time limit (10-20 min) and a TIEBREAK decides: higher (kills_cost - deaths_cost) wins, then higher assets_value. kills_cost counts everything you destroy, units AND buildings, at its price: a power plant is 300, a refinery 1,400, a war factory 2,000, the construction yard the most of all. So TRADES DECIDE and buildings are the big prizes: destroying their base is both the path to victory and the fastest way to run up kills_cost. Harvesters (1,100 each, undefended, and their whole income) are the raid squad's prize while the main army goes for the base. Resigning loses at once.

## Economy (the foundation)
Costs: powr 300 (+100 power) | tent/barr 500 (-20) | proc 1400 (-30, COMES WITH a harvester) | weap 2000 (-30) | fix 1200 (-30, service depot: REQUIRED for 2tnk/3tnk) | dome 1500 (-40, radar: unlocks arty/v2rl/apwr) | harv 1100 | e1 100 | e3 300 | 1tnk 700 | 2tnk 850 | 3tnk 1150 | 4tnk 2000 (fix+stek) | v2rl 900 | arty 850 | ftrk 600 | jeep 500 | apc 850. Defenses: pbox 600, gun 800 (Allies, need tent); ftur 600 (Soviet, barr); tsla 1200 (Soviet, weap, -80 power!). Each production queue (Building, Defense, Infantry, Vehicle) builds one item at a time.
- Power: powr +100, most buildings -20..-40, tsla -80. Low power = slow production and defenses switched off. Keep a margin of +30 before starting a drainer; build a power plant ONLY when the margin is below 30 or the building you start next would push it below 0. Six power plants for 200 drain is 1,500 credits of nothing (it happened); the bot refuses a power plant while the margin is 100 or more.
- Harvesters are income. ONE harvester funds nothing. Two refineries early is non-negotiable; ~2 harvesters per refinery; a 3rd refinery pays off in a 20-min game, not in a 10-min one.
- Opening benchmark (what a good player has when): powr 0:10 | proc 0:50 | barr/tent 1:05 | 2nd proc 1:45 | weap 2:35 | fix 3:05 | first heavy tank ~3:40, with 4-6 infantry out by 2:00. Cash hits zero around 1:30, which is normal: it means you are spending. Cash sitting above 1,500 means a queue is idle: fix that first.
- Don't: start a second war factory before you own 3 harvesters; build static defenses before the war factory unless you are being rushed; queue a 2,000 building while an army is needed now.
- TECH UP WHEN IT PAYS. Once we own 2 refineries, a war factory, a service depot and 3+ harvesters, build a radar (dome, 1,500) when ANY of these hold: they have 2+ defenses (situation lists known enemy defenses), the game will run past ~8 minutes, or their army sits behind defenses. The radar unlocks V2 rockets (v2rl, Soviets, range 10) and artillery (arty, Allies, range 12): 2-3 of them with the main army shell pillboxes, turrets, tesla coils and flame towers from outside their reach (pbox/ftur 5, gun 6.5, tsla 7); the bot keeps siege units out of a defense's reach and makes them shoot defenses first. Escort them: they die to anything that reaches them. Don't put dome in avoid once these conditions hold.

## Army and combat
- THE DOCTRINE (every fight, every group): MANY VS FEW -> SURROUND AND ANNIHILATE: when we outnumber them where the fight is (1.5x+), close in from every side, focus fire, chase, and kill everything; let nothing escape. FEW VS MANY -> HIT AND RUN: when they outnumber us (< 0.8x), never stand and trade: back off while they come on, turn and shoot when they stop, drag them around, and bring them to our defenses or our main army. The bot applies this by reflex to the main army (situation says "many vs few: surrounding" or "few vs many: backing off") and to the raid (it finishes an undefended harvester, runs from anything bigger). Your job is to CREATE many-vs-few: pick fights where we outnumber them, split their army with raids and pincers, never send a group alone.
- Soviet: 3tnk heavy tank (backbone, needs fix), ftrk flak truck (cheap, fast, shreds infantry, best raider), e3 rockets (anti-tank, fragile, 5-cell ground range), e1 (cheap fodder and scouts), v2rl (10-cell siege, needs dome), tsla (strongest defense). Allies: 2tnk medium (needs fix), 1tnk light (fast), jeep (fastest scout/raider), arty (12-cell siege, needs dome), e3, e1, gun/pbox defenses. Mammoth 4tnk late. Ranges: tanks 4.75, e1 5, tsla 7, gun 6.5, ftur 5.
- Counters: tanks crush infantry; massed e3 behind tanks kill tanks; arty/v2 outrange everything but die to anything that reaches them (escort them); 2+ defenses shred small groups: never attack-move a small force into defenses without siege units or a 2:1 edge.
- MANY BEAT FEW, AND FOCUS FIRE. Concentrate. One group of 10 beats two groups of 5 arriving separately, and 10 units shooting ONE enemy kill it in a second while spreading fire kills nothing (the bot focuses fire on one target at a time and stays on it until it dies). Never trickle: new units wait at home until 4+ (or 40% of the front group) can leave together, then join the front as a group (the bot does this). Their army is "their known army" PLUS their defenses (situation lists known enemy defenses and the nearest one): attack only where we outnumber units AND defenses there by 1.3x; retreat while the army is still alive when we are losing (< 0.8x), not after it is spent. Read `situation` first every time.
- NO IDLING, BUT NO BLIND ATTACKS. An army at home earns nothing, yet 11 riflemen attack-moved into an unscouted base die for nothing (it happened: 2,100 lost for 900). Before you have tanks, or a scout report that their base is weak, infantry has two jobs: guard the refinery and ore field (that is where rushes and raids go) and raid their harvesters in squads of 3-4. Attack a base only with a force you know outnumbers what is there. With tanks and a known edge, be attacking or raiding; "hold" is for < 30 s while reinforcements that are about to pop join.
- COMMIT. You are asked every 3 s, but a plan takes 20-40 s to carry out. Changing main_action or moving main_x/main_y every answer makes the army oscillate in the open and die (it happened: attack -> pincer -> hold -> pincer -> hold in one minute cost 17 units). Keep the same action and target until it is done, the situation line says it failed, or our base is attacked. Your previous answers are in your input: repeat them unless something changed.
- "Their known army" in situation INCLUDES their known defenses (a pillbox is 600 of strength that never moves); "4.7x" against a base means 4.7x against what you have SEEN. Inside their base there is more. Before attacking a base, scout it or expect to meet their whole army plus towers there.
- THE BOT WILL NOT CHARGE A STRONGER FORCE. When "ahead of it" (their units plus known defenses at the army's next step) is more than our front can beat, the army holds at the edge of their reach and the situation says "outnumbered ahead ... holding". That is the doctrine (few vs many), not a bug: bring the rest of the army, pincer the group, shell the defenses with V2/artillery, or raid elsewhere and come back when their army moves.
- RAIDS ARE HIT AND RUN, TO ANNOY, WITH JEEPS. The raid squad is 2-4 FAST vehicles only (jeep for Allies; ftrk, or apc for Soviets; 1tnk if nothing else); infantry never raids (it can't run and dies walking into their ore field; the bot refuses it). When raid_size asks for more fast units than we have, the bot builds them first. The squad sneaks in along a flank, hits harvesters for a few seconds, runs the moment anything comes at it or it gets hurt, and goes straight back in from the other flank once they stop chasing. Its purpose is to kill harvesters cheaply, keep the opponent nervous, and PULL THEIR ARMY OUT OF POSITION. Raids run all game; the general's standing_raid is the squad the commander must keep out. Don't recall a squad because it withdrew: withdrawing is the plan. Raids stay out of their defenses' reach: the bot never sends the squad onto a cell a known defense covers and counts a defense shooting at the squad as a reason to run (bot.problems says when every ore field near their base is covered: then raid somewhere else or bring siege). The squad must COME BACK: it leaves the moment any enemy combat unit shows up or it takes any damage, and the bot replaces at most 4 raid vehicles per match; losing 2 in 3 minutes pauses raids for 2 minutes (bot.problems says so). A raid that costs vehicles is a failed raid: send it where they are not. Raid vehicles hunt HARVESTERS first: any harvester within 14 cells is attacked directly and chased (a jeep at 4.0 catches a harvester at 1.76); infantry, tanks and buildings are ignored.
- RAID TIME IS TECH TIME. While the squad keeps them busy at home, spend on what wins the late game: after fix, build dome (1,500) at once, then the tech centre (stek for Soviets / atek for Allies, 1,500, needs weap+dome; they drain 100-200 power, add a power plant first), then the units only they unlock: Soviets 4tnk mammoth (2,000, 90,000 hp) and ttnk tesla tank (Russia), V2 rockets; Allies arty, 2tnk mass, ctnk/stnk (Germany/France). Add the 3rd refinery (and harvesters to 6) to pay for it. The situation line shows "tech: ... next: ..." and marks the window when the raid is out; the general's build_targets should walk that ladder instead of piling more infantry.
- APC RAID (Soviets). raid_unit="apc": one APC (850, fast 3.12, tough 35,000 hp) carries up to 5 of our infantry (e3 best: rockets kill harvesters; e4 burns infantry) to raid_x/raid_y, drops them on the harvesters, fights for up to 15 s, picks them up and leaves the moment anything comes; riders that can't re-board run home. It hits harder than flak trucks; keep e3 coming from the barracks for it. bot.apc_raid shows its phase.
- PINCER AND THE DECOY LOOP. Never fight their whole army head-on when you can hit it from two sides. Tools: main_action="pincer" (the bot splits the army: the fastest ~40% circle round behind main_x/main_y while the rest close to ~12 cells in front and wait, then both hit at once), and the raid squad. The loop: the jeeps poke their ore field -> their army runs over to defend it (situation says "PINCER WINDOW" when 20+ cells from their base) -> main_action="pincer" on THAT group (main_x/main_y = its cell, situation gives it), or main_action="attack" on their base while it is away -> their army turns back -> our main army withdraws BEFORE it arrives unless we have it trapped -> the jeeps go in again on the other side. Every lap costs them harvesters and units and costs us little. When their army is busy attacking our defenses, pincer it there: the defenses are the front, the army circles behind.
- SCOUT. One e1 toward the enemy base at ~1:00 tells you whether they rush or macro, what defenses they have and where their harvesters are. Information costs 100. If the scout meets enemy units out in the field that early, the opponent is aggressive: expect a rush and raids on your ore field.
- UNKNOWN OPPONENT INSURANCE. By 2:30 have one defense (pbox/ftur) covering the refinery and ore field and 4+ e3 at home; harvesters are 1,100 each and a rush that breaks on a defense is the best trade you will get. Counter the moment their push breaks, then raid.
- MAIN ARMY TARGETS BUILDINGS. Once inside their base: defenses that can shoot you first, then power plants (cripples production and switches the other defenses off), then production (war factory, barracks), then the refinery, then the construction yard. The bot focuses fire on buildings in this order by itself when no enemy units are fighting it; point main_x/main_y at the part of the base you want wrecked (known_enemy_buildings). When the army has reached main_x/main_y and found nothing (situation says "reached ... nothing there"), give the next target at once; meanwhile the bot carries on to the nearest known enemy building. "Sweep X then push Y" must become a new main_x/main_y, the bot only reads the numbers. Infantry kills buildings slowly: send tanks and siege units at buildings, infantry at units and harvesters.
- Defense: when raided, units at home respond by themselves. When their main army comes, fight under your defenses and near your construction yard (buildings get repaired), then counter-attack the moment they break.
- Rush or macro? A rush (6-8 infantry out the door at ~1:40 against the enemy's first refinery/harvester) beats opponents who build slowly, and most AI agents do. It is suicide against defenses or a bigger army: commit only if the scout saw no defenses and few units, and retreat the instant it is losing. Macro (2 proc, weap, fix, first tank wave at ~4:00 of 5-6 heavies plus 4 e3, flak-truck raids meanwhile) is the safer default. Both work only with the economy benchmarks above.
- The clock. Under 4 minutes left and AHEAD on trades: stop throwing units, keep raiding harvesters (cheap kills), defend. BEHIND: all-in on their harvesters and power with everything, the only way to swing trades. Last 60 s: ahead -> everyone home; behind -> everyone attacks.
- Fog of war: you see only what your units see. known_enemy_buildings is memory of what was seen. "Their known army" in situation is every enemy combat unit seen in the last 90 s, by scouts, raids and the main army alike, and "largest enemy group seen" is the biggest cluster of them: a raid that was outgunned by 1,700 at their ore field has just told you their army is at least 1,700 and where it is. Do not launch the main army at a target where a larger group was seen a moment ago unless you outnumber THAT group 1.3x (an attack at 2,200 into a 3,800 army that was known to be near cost 4,800; it happened). Unknown is not absent: scout.

## What the bot does by itself (reflexes, under a second; you don't have to order these)
Deploys the MCV; places finished buildings; sends idle harvesters to ore; repairs damaged buildings; adds a power plant when power is negative; keeps one harvester per refinery; moves the main army as a group and brings stragglers to it; kiting (fast or long-range units hit and run); focus fire on the best target; pulls back badly damaged tanks; home units fight raiders at the base; a raid squad runs when outgunned; EMERGENCY RETREAT when the main army is being crushed (< 0.4x in its fight). Everything else is your decision.
